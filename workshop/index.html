Workshop

Small sites. Big curiosity.

What happens when an idea can become something you can actually click, starting with a WhatsApp message?

The Workshop is where I find out. It's a growing collection of small sites, useful tools and experiments: a reading room for my papers, emergency-overview demos, and examples of automating everyday research and monitoring.

My original curiosity was simple: how much could I do through a messaging app I already use? WhatsApp is where I ask questions, share ideas and give feedback. No new app or extra login for that everyday conversation, just a familiar place to start turning ideas into working solutions almost immediately.

I bring the questions and direction. My AI assistant, Instinct, helps turn them into working pages through design, writing and code. I test, challenge and refine what comes back.

For the work behind those conversations, I chose to set up separate accounts for Instinct - such as Google, GitHub and genealogy research sites. That took some initial setup, but meant I could give it its own workspace and share selected project material, rather than hand over my own account logins.

Some projects are practical. Some are prototypes. All are a way to learn by making, rather than just talking about what technology might do. Demonstrations using fictional data are labelled, and private research files aren't published here.

Take a look around. You might find something useful, or the beginning of your next idea.
